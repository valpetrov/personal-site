ABOUT

Most of my work has been behind the scenes.

I’ve worked on trade booking at Point72, built a security master at Centiva, led development of a portfolio platform at Schonfeld processing more than 6 million transactions a day, and built Bloomberg services handling hundreds of millions of messages daily.

The work ranges from instrument creation and trade processing to portfolio calculations and reporting. It usually involves old systems, new requirements, large data volumes, and little room for mistakes. I like making those systems easier to understand, operate, and change.

Lately I’ve been applying that experience to LLM-based tools, including agent workflows, retrieval, and tool use. I’m interested in what holds up outside a demo: useful results, understandable failures, and a clear way for people to stay in control.
